# Contributor Code of Conduct

The UthyAgent project aims to provide a respectful, welcoming environment for everyone.

Examples of expected behavior include being considerate, giving and receiving constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the project, and respecting differing experiences and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, and sustained disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers. They will review reports fairly and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. The project is an alpha; its public enforcement contact and reporting process must be completed before a public community launch.
